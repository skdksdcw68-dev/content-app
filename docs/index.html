It plans the month. You approve it. It posts.

Netro Autocast is a content agent for people who know they should be posting every day and are not. You tell it what your account is about; it decides what to make, makes it, writes the caption, books the slot, and publishes.

How it works

  • 1 Tell it about you Who the account is for, what it is about, the voice it should write in, and the hours it must never post in.
  • 2 Ask for a plan Up to thirty days at once. Every post says, in one line, why it chose that idea — so you are never looking at something that appeared for no reason.
  • 3 Approve what you want published You see the caption, the media and the privacy setting for every post, alongside the account it will go out from.
  • 4 It posts on schedule Daily, at the times you approved, whether or not the app is open.

Nothing is published without your permission. Approval is captured per post, against the exact caption and the exact media that will go out. If either changes afterwards, the post stops and comes back to you rather than publishing something you did not agree to.

What it makes

Media is generated by whichever provider you connect with your own account, or licensed from a stock library, or uploaded by you. Netro Autocast records where every asset came from and will refuse to publish one whose rights are unclear. Images found elsewhere can guide the style of what it generates; they are never republished as your content.

Where it posts

TikTok first, with Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts to follow. You connect each account yourself, and you can disconnect it at any time — which stops all future posting to it immediately.

Status

Netro Autocast is in active development and testing through TestFlight. If you would like to try it, or you have questions about how it handles your accounts and your data, write to hello@netrocast.com.
