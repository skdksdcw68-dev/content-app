Support

Write to hello@netrocast.com and a person will answer, usually within a day. In the app, Profile → Contact support opens an email with your app version already filled in.

Connecting accounts

  • TikTok: Profile → Accounts → TikTok → Connect. While TikTok reviews Netro Autocast, it only accepts direct posts from private accounts; sending to your TikTok drafts works on any account.
  • YouTube: Profile → Accounts → YouTube → Connect, and sign in with the Google account that owns your channel.
  • Instagram: Profile → Accounts → Instagram → Connect. Instagram only lets apps post from Business or Creator accounts (Instagram → Settings → Account type and tools).

A post didn't go out

Open the post. It shows what happened and why, in plain words — for example a platform's daily limit, an account that needs reconnecting, or a video the platform couldn't process. Nothing is ever posted without your approval, and a post that changed after you approved it waits for you to approve it again.

Autocast Pro

  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To cancel or change plan: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Autocast Pro.
  • Moved to a new phone? Profile → Get Autocast Pro → Restore.
  • Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

  • Export: Profile → Export my data.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete Account removes your account and everything in it, and disconnects every platform.
  • Details in the Privacy Policy.
